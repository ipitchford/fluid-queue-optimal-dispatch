# Sources and their roles

The manuscript's reference list has 24 entries, all with DOIs (checked in [CITATION_AUDIT.md](CITATION_AUDIT.md)). The table records how deeply each load-bearing source was read. It follows the manuscript, the bounded novelty search of 19 October 2026, the internal review records and the adversarial audit of 23 October 2026.

Rev. 53(4) (2026) 42–48, doi:10.1145/3797823.3797843 ("XHR") | The target. (3) The 23 October verification re-review read the same issue copy (matching SHA-256) and found the conjecture wording, Prop. 3 and the most-work-left definition identical to arXiv v1.
